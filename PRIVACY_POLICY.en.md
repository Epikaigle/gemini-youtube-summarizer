# Privacy policy

[Français](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) · [English](PRIVACY_POLICY.en.md) · [Español](PRIVACY_POLICY.es.md)

## YouTube Summarizer with Gemini

The extension does not send any data to the developer. It has no data collection server, telemetry, or user accounts.

## Action on YouTube

When you explicitly click **Summarize**, the extension retrieves the selected video’s URL, reads your locally stored instructions, and opens `https://gemini.google.com/` with this information. It attempts to fill Gemini’s input and send the message. If you disable automatic submission in preferences, the message is left ready to send manually.

## Data sent to Gemini

- The URL of the YouTube video you chose to summarize.
- The instructions configured in the extension’s preferences, if any.

This information is sent to Gemini only to carry out the action you requested.

## Local storage

Your instructions, extension language, and automatic submission setting are kept in Firefox’s local storage. These preferences are not sent to the developer. Your instructions are sent to Gemini when you explicitly start a request.

## No data collection by the developer

The extension does not manage user accounts, keep a history, or add cookies. It produces no statistics or telemetry and does not send any content to a developer server.

## Third-party service and user control

Gemini is a Google service. Its use is subject to Google’s terms and privacy policy. Signing in to a Google account may be required to use Gemini.

Nothing is sent to Gemini without your action on **Summarize**. You can change your preferences, reset settings, or uninstall the extension at any time.
